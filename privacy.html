Privacy Policy

youtube-api-board is a private tool used only by the owner of the connected YouTube channel(s). It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

With your consent, the tool reads your channel and video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, thumbnails), channel analytics and reporting data, and updates video metadata and thumbnails when you run it.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to show you your own analytics and to apply the changes you request. OAuth tokens, analytics results and backups of previous metadata are stored only on your own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs, and no data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or via the Google security settings page. Deleting the tool's local folder removes all stored data.
